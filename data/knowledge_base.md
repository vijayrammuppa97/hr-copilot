# 1. Leave Policy

## 1.1 Annual Leave Overview

All permanent full-time employees are entitled to paid annual leave from their first day of employment. Leave accrues monthly and may be used for vacation, personal days, or any personal reason at the employee's discretion. Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rated basis relative to their contracted hours.

Annual leave must be pre-approved by the direct manager except in genuine emergencies. Requests should be submitted via the HR Portal (hrportal.acme.com) with reasonable advance notice.

## 1.2 Annual Leave Days by Tenure

Leave entitlement increases with years of continuous service:

- **0 to 2 years of service:** 15 days per year (1.25 days accrued per month)
- **2 to 5 years of service:** 18 days per year (1.5 days accrued per month)
- **5 to 10 years of service:** 22 days per year (approximately 1.83 days per month)
- **10 or more years of service:** 25 days per year (approximately 2.08 days per month)

Accrual pauses during unpaid leave periods exceeding 14 consecutive calendar days.

## 1.3 Annual Leave Request Process

To apply for annual leave:
1. Log in to the HR Portal and navigate to Leave Management.
2. Select the dates and submit the request at least **5 business days** in advance for absences of 1–3 days.
3. For absences of 4 or more consecutive days, submit at least **10 business days** in advance.
4. The direct manager will approve or decline within 3 business days.
5. Approved leave is automatically deducted from the employee's balance.

For urgent situations (e.g., family emergencies), notify your manager by phone or message immediately, then formalise in the HR Portal within 48 hours of returning to work.

## 1.4 Annual Leave Carry-Forward Rules

Unused annual leave may be carried into the next calendar year under the following limits:

- **Standard carry-forward:** Up to 5 unused days may roll over to January 1 of the following year.
- **Exceptional carry-forward:** Up to 10 days with written approval from both the manager and HR Business Partner (e.g., employee was unable to take leave due to a critical project or illness).
- Any leave balance exceeding the approved carry-forward limit is forfeited on December 31.

Employees are strongly encouraged to take a minimum of 10 days per calendar year to support wellbeing and prevent burnout.

## 1.5 Annual Leave During Notice Period

Employees who resign or are made redundant may take accrued annual leave during the notice period, subject to manager approval. Any remaining accrued leave balance at termination is paid out in the final paycheck, up to the carry-forward maximum.

---

## 1.6 Sick Leave Policy

Acme provides paid sick leave to support employee health and recovery without financial penalty.

**Entitlement:**
- Full-time employees: **10 paid sick days per calendar year**
- Part-time employees: Pro-rated based on contracted hours
- Sick leave does not roll over; unused days are forfeited on December 31 each year

**Permitted uses:**
- Personal illness or injury (physical or mental health)
- Medical, dental, or mental health appointments
- Recovery from surgery or chronic condition flare-up
- Caring for an ill immediate family member (up to **3 days per year** as Family Sick Leave)

## 1.7 Sick Leave Documentation Requirements

- **1–2 consecutive sick days:** No medical documentation required. Self-certification in the HR Portal is sufficient.
- **3 or more consecutive sick days:** A medical certificate from a registered healthcare provider must be submitted to HR within 5 business days of returning to work.
- **Repeated short-term absence:** Employees with 8 or more separate sick leave episodes in a rolling 12-month period may be invited to a supportive review meeting with HR and their manager to explore whether additional support, workplace adjustments, or an occupational health referral would be beneficial.

## 1.8 Mental Health Days

Employees may designate up to **2 sick days per year as mental health days** without providing a medical certificate, regardless of the general sick leave documentation requirements. These are taken from the standard 10-day sick leave balance. Employees are not required to disclose the reason beyond "mental health day."

---

## 1.9 Maternity Leave

Birthing parents are entitled to up to **26 weeks of maternity leave**:

- **Weeks 1–18:** Fully paid at 100% of base salary
- **Weeks 19–26:** Paid at 60% of base salary

**Key rules:**
- Leave may begin up to 6 weeks before the expected due date.
- If birth occurs after the expected date, pre-natal leave continues until birth without reducing post-natal entitlement.
- Employees must notify HR and their manager of their intended leave start date at least **8 weeks before** the planned start.
- A letter from a healthcare provider confirming the expected due date must be submitted when notifying HR.
- Employees may return earlier than planned with 4 weeks' written notice to their manager and HR.

## 1.10 Paternity Leave

Non-birthing parents (fathers, same-sex partners, secondary caregivers) are entitled to:

- **2 weeks fully paid paternity leave**, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption placement
- An optional **2 additional weeks unpaid**, taken immediately after the paid period

**How to apply:**
1. Submit a paternity leave request via the HR Portal at least 4 weeks before the intended start date (or as soon as reasonably possible after the birth).
2. Provide a birth registration certificate or evidence of adoption placement upon return to work.

## 1.11 Shared Parental Leave

Following the first 6 weeks of compulsory maternity leave, both parents employed by Acme may share remaining leave:

- Total paid leave across both parents cannot exceed 26 weeks at 100% pay (weeks 1–18) and 60% (weeks 19–26).
- Leave may be taken concurrently or in alternating blocks.
- Submit a Shared Parental Leave Agreement form to HR at least 8 weeks before the first block begins.
- Both employees must confirm their respective employers, eligibility, and block dates in writing.

## 1.12 Adoption Leave

Employees adopting a child are entitled to leave equivalent to maternity leave (up to 26 weeks, same pay schedule). Leave begins on the date of the child's placement with the adopting family. Only one adoptive parent may take adoption leave; the other may take paternity leave.

## 1.13 Special Leaves

### Bereavement Leave

Paid bereavement leave upon the death of a family member:

- **Immediate family** (spouse/partner, child, parent, sibling): Up to **5 paid days**
- **Extended family** (grandparent, grandchild, parent-in-law, aunt/uncle): Up to **3 paid days**
- **Close friend or colleague** (at manager's discretion): Up to **1 paid day**

Additional unpaid leave may be approved for travel, cultural ceremonies, or estate administration. A copy of an obituary, death notice, or death certificate may be requested for absences beyond 5 days.

### Emergency Personal Leave

Up to **2 days per year** for genuine personal emergencies (e.g., sudden home flooding, acute domestic crisis) without advance notice:
- Notify your manager as soon as possible (text or email acceptable).
- Record the leave in the HR Portal within 48 hours of returning to work.
- Deducted from annual leave balance; if exhausted, it is unpaid.

### Study and Exam Leave

- **Exam leave:** Up to **2 paid days per exam** per calendar year for job-relevant qualifications.
- **Study leave:** Up to **5 paid days per year** for professional development courses, conferences, or certifications, subject to manager and budget approval.
- Submit requests 4 weeks in advance with evidence of enrolment or registration.

### Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Employees called for jury duty or to perform other legally required civic obligations (voting, official witness duties) receive paid leave for the duration. Provide the official summons to HR before the absence.

### Unpaid Leave of Absence

Employees who have exhausted paid entitlements may apply for unpaid leave for personal, medical, or family reasons. Submit a written request to your manager and HR at least 2 weeks in advance. Unpaid leave up to 12 weeks maintains service continuity; longer periods may affect benefit accruals and pension contributions.

# 2. Remote Work Policy

## 2.1 Remote Work Overview

Acme supports flexible working arrangements that balance employee autonomy with team collaboration and business needs. "Remote work" includes both fully remote roles and hybrid arrangements where an employee works from home on some days and from an office on others.

## 2.2 Who Is Eligible for Remote Work

Remote or hybrid work is available to employees whose roles can be performed effectively outside the office. Eligibility requires:

- The role does not require a regular, unavoidable on-site physical presence (e.g., laboratory work, reception, facilities management, on-site manufacturing).
- The employee has completed at least **3 months of continuous employment** at Acme.
- The employee's most recent performance rating is **"Meets Expectations" or above**.
- The employee has a suitable, secure home working environment (see Section 2.5).

**Not eligible:** Employees currently on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are not eligible for remote work arrangements until the PIP is successfully completed.

## 2.3 Remote Work Days Allowed Per Week

Approved arrangements vary by role and team agreement:

- **Hybrid — standard:** Up to **3 days remote per week** (2 days minimum in-office)
- **Hybrid — flexible:** Up to **4 days remote per week** for roles with low in-person dependency, approved by VP-level or above
- **Fully remote:** Available for specific roles designated as fully remote at hiring; or by exception with Director and HR approval
- **Ad hoc remote days:** Occasional unplanned remote days are permitted with same-day manager notification, without requiring a formal agreement, provided team norms allow it

In-office presence is expected on days designated as team collaboration days by the manager (typically 1–2 fixed days per week per team).

## 2.4 How to Apply for a Formal Remote Work Arrangement

1. Complete the **Remote Work Agreement Form** on the HR Portal.
2. Discuss and agree the schedule (remote days, core hours, communication norms) with your direct manager.
3. Both parties sign the agreement and submit it to HR for review.
4. HR will confirm approval or request modifications within **10 business days**.
5. The arrangement is reviewed **annually** or upon change of role, team, or performance status.

## 2.5 Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely must maintain a safe, productive, and professional home workspace:

- **Dedicated workspace:** A reasonably quiet area with a door or partition, free from significant background disturbance during core working hours.
- **Ergonomics:** An adjustable chair and desk at appropriate heights. Acme provides a one-time **$500 home office setup allowance** for employees on a formal remote agreement (submit receipts to HR within 90 days of agreement approval; see Benefits Section 4.3).
- **Internet speed:** Minimum **25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload** for stable video conferencing.
- **IT security compliance:** Full compliance with the Acme IT Security Policy at all times (VPN always on, encrypted disk, MFA enabled — see Section 2.7).

Acme reserves the right to conduct a virtual ergonomic assessment if a work-related physical complaint is reported by a remote employee.

## 2.6 Equipment Provided to Remote Employees

Acme provides all equipment necessary for effective remote work:

| Equipment | Provided by Acme | Employee Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop (Windows or Mac, role-dependent) | Yes | Return in good condition on exit |
| Noise-cancelling headset | Yes | Report faults within 48 hours |
| External monitor (upon request) | Yes, for formal remote agreements | Not to be used for personal purposes |
| VPN software and licence | Yes | Must be active whenever accessing company systems |
| MFA app (e.g., Microsoft Authenticator) | Yes | Must not be removed or bypassed |
| Web camera | Yes (if not built into laptop) | Maintain for client/team video calls |

Employees must **not use personal devices** for work involving sensitive company data, client records, or confidential information unless explicitly approved in writing by IT Security. Lost or stolen company equipment must be reported to IT Security within **4 hours** of discovery.

## 2.6.1 Lost or Stolen Company Equipment — Reporting Procedure

If your company laptop, phone, or any Acme-issued device is **lost or stolen**, you must:

1. **Report immediately to IT Security** at it-security@acme.com within **4 hours** of discovering the loss.
2. Contact your **direct manager** as soon as possible (text or email is acceptable).
3. File a **police report** if the device was stolen and share the reference number with IT Security.
4. IT Security will remotely wipe the device and revoke access credentials within 2 hours of your report.
5. Do **not** attempt to recover the device yourself or delay reporting — unauthorised delay may result in disciplinary action.

**Replacement:** A replacement device will be issued within 2 business days. Employees are not financially liable for theft if reported within the 4-hour window, provided reasonable care was taken.

**Source:** Acme IT Security Policy, Section 2.6

## 2.7 Internet, Security, and Connectivity Requirements

**Network security:**
- Home Wi-Fi must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption. Default router passwords must be changed before working remotely.
- The corporate VPN must be active for all access to internal systems, Acme Drive, and HR Portal.
- Public or shared Wi-Fi (cafes, hotels, airports) must **never** be used for company work without the VPN enabled.

**Broadband subsidy:**
- Employees on a formal remote agreement receive a **$50 per month broadband subsidy**, automatically added to payroll.

**Connectivity issues:**
- If internet connectivity is unreliable or fails during a working day, notify your manager immediately and work from the nearest Acme office if possible.
- Persistent connectivity problems that affect performance must be resolved within 30 days or the remote arrangement may be suspended pending resolution.

## 2.8 Working Hours and Availability for Remote Workers

Remote employees are held to the same performance standards as in-office employees. Expectations:

- Be available and responsive during **core hours of 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM in your local time zone**, Monday to Friday.
- Respond to Slack messages within **2 business hours** and emails within **4 business hours** during core hours.
- Attend all required synchronous meetings (video on for client-facing and team stand-ups unless agreed otherwise).
- Do not regularly work more than **10 hours per day**; raise workload concerns with your manager.
- Non-exempt (hourly) employees must log all working hours in the Time Tracking portal regardless of location.

## 2.9 Remote Work Performance Standards

Remote work is a privilege, not a right. Managers monitor output and quality in the same way as in-office employees:

- Managers conduct at least **one video check-in per week** with fully remote employees.
- Underperformance in a remote arrangement will trigger a review; the arrangement may be modified or revoked with **2 weeks' written notice**.
- Employees may request a return to in-office work at any time with 1 week's notice to their manager.

## 2.10 International Remote Work

Working from a country other than the employee's contracted country of employment requires prior written approval from HR, Legal, and Finance. Unapproved international remote work may create unexpected tax liabilities for the employee and the company, and may violate local employment laws. Submit requests at least **6 weeks in advance**. Blanket approvals are not given; each request is assessed individually.

# 3. Onboarding Process

## 3.1 Onboarding Overview

Acme's onboarding programme is designed to help new employees feel welcome, productive, and connected from their very first day. The programme spans 90 days and involves the employee, their direct manager, an assigned buddy, HR, and IT.

## 3.2 Pre-boarding (Before Day 1)

The following steps occur before the employee's first day, coordinated by HR and the Hiring Manager:

- Signed employment contract and right-to-work documentation received and verified by HR.
- IT account creation request submitted by the Hiring Manager at least **5 business days** before the start date.
- Company laptop prepared and either shipped to the employee's address (remote) or available for collection on Day 1 (in-office).
- Welcome email sent by HR at least 3 days before start, including: Day 1 schedule, office address and parking/access instructions, IT setup steps, HR Portal login details, and the buddy's name and contact.
- **Buddy assigned** from the employee's immediate team (a peer with at least 1 year of tenure, not the direct manager).
- Benefits enrollment window opened in the HR Portal (employees have 30 days from start date to enroll).

## 3.3 Day 1 Setup and Schedule

Day 1 is structured to cover essential logistics without overwhelming the new employee:

**Morning:**
- 9:00 AM — Welcome from HR: office tour (or virtual tour for remote), introductions to the People & Culture team, safety briefing.
- 9:30 AM — IT setup session: laptop configuration, company email activation, Slack workspace join, VPN installation, MFA setup, HR Portal registration.
- 10:30 AM — Introduction to direct manager: role overview, team structure, immediate priorities.
- 11:00 AM — Team introduction meeting (video call for distributed teams).

**Afternoon:**
- 12:00 PM — Lunch with buddy (in-person or virtual coffee chat).
- 1:30 PM — HR orientation: benefits enrollment walkthrough, payroll setup (direct deposit), leave policy, Code of Conduct acknowledgement, mandatory compliance training assignment in the Learning Management System (LMS).
- 3:00 PM — Manager-led role-specific onboarding begins: tools, workflows, key contacts.
- 4:30 PM — End-of-day check-in with manager: any questions, plan for Day 2.

**Before end of Day 1, the employee must complete:**
- Direct deposit / payroll setup in HR Portal
- Emergency contact submission in HR Portal
- IT security acknowledgement form
- Code of Conduct digital signature

## 3.4 First Week Activities (Days 2–5)

During the first week, the new employee should:

- Complete all **mandatory compliance training** in the LMS (typically 4–6 courses covering data privacy, anti-harassment, information security, and health & safety). Deadline: end of Week 2.
- Schedule **30-minute introductory meetings** ("get to know you" calls) with at least 8 key stakeholders: team members, cross-functional partners, and internal clients identified by the manager.
- Review **team ways-of-working documentation**: team norms, communication channels, meeting cadence, project management tools (Jira, Notion, etc.).
- Begin learning key tools and systems relevant to the role, with a buddy or manager guiding setup.
- Join relevant Slack channels and mailing lists as identified by the manager.
- Review the Acme product/service overview materials shared by the manager.
- **Benefits enrollment:** Enroll in health, dental, vision, and retirement plans. Enrollment must be completed within **30 days** of start date; late enrollment requires a qualifying life event.
- Meet with buddy at least once (informal, off-agenda — buddy's role is to answer unwritten-rules questions).

## 3.5 First Month Timeline (Days 6–30)

**Week 2:**
- Complete any outstanding mandatory LMS training.
- Take ownership of a first task or project deliverable (identified by manager in Week 1).
- Attend all regular team rituals (stand-ups, planning, retrospectives).
- First formal 1:1 with manager: discuss initial impressions, any blockers, clarity on goals.

**Week 3:**
- Begin contributing independently to at least one team project.
- Shadow a senior colleague on a client call, design review, or key workflow.
- Identify any skill or knowledge gaps; discuss a development plan with the manager.

**Week 4:**
- Contribute a meaningful deliverable (code shipped, analysis published, proposal drafted — role-dependent).
- Complete the **30-day check-in** with manager: informal review of progress, clarity of role, and any support needed.
- Complete the **30-day new hire survey** in the HR Portal (anonymous feedback used to improve the onboarding experience).

## 3.6 First Month Timeline continued — Days 31–90

**Days 31–60 (Month 2):**
- Take full ownership of core responsibilities in the role.
- Agree on the **60-day milestone goals** with the manager (specific, measurable outcomes).
- Complete a mid-probation review at **6 weeks**: informal written assessment by manager, shared with employee and filed in HR.
- Identify and enrol in one internal or external learning course relevant to the role.
- Build working relationships with at least 3 cross-functional partners.

**Days 61–90 (Month 3):**
- Operate independently across all core responsibilities.
- Deliver the agreed 60-day milestone outcomes.
- Complete the formal **90-day probation review**:
  - Manager completes a probation assessment form in Workday and shares it with the employee at least 3 days before the meeting.
  - A meeting is held between employee and manager (HR invited if there are concerns).
  - Outcome: Pass (probation confirmed), Extend (up to 6 additional weeks with written reasons and HR approval), or Fail (begin exit process with HR).
- Agree on **90-day goals** for the next quarter using the OKR framework.
- Complete the **90-day new hire survey** in the HR Portal.

## 3.7 Buddy Programme Details

The buddy is a peer (not a line manager) who helps the new employee settle in socially and culturally:

- Assigned before Day 1; introduction email sent by HR.
- Expected to meet informally with the new hire at least **twice in the first month** and stay available throughout the first 90 days.
- Answers questions about culture, unwritten norms, and processes without the formality of manager conversations.
- Does not assess performance or report conversations to management.
- Employees who wish to become buddies can self-nominate on the HR Portal → Buddy Programme.

## 3.8 Probationary Period Summary

All new employees serve a **3-month probationary period**:

- Notice period during probation: **1 week** (either party).
- Remote work arrangements are not available during the probationary period.
- Weekly 1:1 check-ins between employee and manager are recommended.
- The formal probation assessment is due by the end of Month 3.
- Probation may be extended up to **6 additional weeks** with written reasons and HR Business Partner approval.

# 4. Benefits

## 4.1 Health Insurance

Acme provides comprehensive health insurance through **CareShield Plus**, effective from the first day of employment.

**Employee coverage:** 100% company-paid premium.

**Dependant coverage (employee + spouse/partner + up to 3 dependent children):**
- Company pays 80% of the premium; the remaining 20% is deducted from the employee's monthly paycheck.

**Plan details:**
- Annual deductible: $500 per individual / $1,500 per family
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,000 per individual / $9,000 per family
- In-network primary care co-pay: $20 per visit
- In-network specialist co-pay: $40 per visit
- Emergency room co-pay: $150 (waived if admitted as inpatient)
- Urgent care co-pay: $50
- Prescription drugs: $10 generic / $30 preferred brand / $60 non-preferred brand (per 30-day supply)
- Preventive care (annual physicals, screenings, vaccinations): 100% covered, no deductible

**Mental health parity:** Mental health and substance use disorder services are covered at the same level as physical health. Teletherapy and in-person therapy are both covered (specialist co-pay applies).

Coverage for new hires begins on the first day of employment. Enroll via the HR Portal within 30 days of start date. Changes outside the enrollment window require a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, divorce, loss of other coverage).

## 4.2 Dental and Vision Insurance

**Dental — DentalGuard Plan:**
- Preventive care (cleanings x2/year, x-rays, oral exams): **100% covered**, no deductible
- Basic restorative (fillings, simple extractions): **80% covered** after a $100 annual deductible
- Major restorative (crowns, root canals, bridges): **50% covered**; annual maximum benefit: $2,000
- Orthodontia (adults and children): **50% covered**; lifetime maximum: $1,500

**Vision — ClearView Plan:**
- Annual comprehensive eye exam: **100% covered**
- Frames allowance: **$200 per year** (in-network; out-of-network reimbursed at $120)
- Contact lenses allowance: **$200 per year** (instead of frames)
- Lens replacement: **$150 per year**

## 4.3 Retirement Plan — 401(k)

Acme offers a 401(k) plan administered by **Fidelity Investments**.

**Eligibility and enrollment:**
- Eligible from the first day of employment.
- Auto-enrolled at 3% contribution after 30 days unless the employee opts out or changes the rate.

**Company match:**
- **100% match on the first 4%** of base salary contributed (immediately fully vested).
- **50% match on the next 2%** of base salary (vests at 25% per year over 4 years; fully vested at Year 4).

**2025 IRS contribution limits:**
- Under age 50: $23,000 per year
- Age 50 and above: $30,500 per year (includes $7,500 catch-up contribution)

**Investment options:** Broad range of index funds, mutual funds, and target-date retirement funds. Manage contributions and investments at fidelity.com/netbenefits or via the Fidelity mobile app.

## 4.4 Life Insurance and Disability

**Life Insurance:**
- Basic life insurance: **2× annual base salary** — fully company-paid, effective Day 1.
- Supplemental life insurance: Up to **5× annual base salary** (employee-paid; evidence of insurability required above 3×).
- AD&D (Accidental Death & Dismemberment): **1× annual base salary** — fully company-paid.

**Short-Term Disability (STD):**
- Covers non-work-related illness or injury.
- Benefit: **80% of base salary** for up to 12 weeks.
- Waiting period: 7 consecutive calendar days.

**Long-Term Disability (LTD):**
- Activates after 12 weeks of continuous disability (when STD ends).
- Benefit: **60% of base salary** until recovery or age 65.
- Company-paid premium; no employee contribution required.

## 4.5 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

The **LifeBalance EAP** is a free, confidential support service for all employees and their immediate household members.

**What's included:**
- 24/7 confidential helpline: **1-800-555-0199**
- Up to **6 free counselling sessions per issue per year** with licensed therapists (in-person or video)

- Work-life referral services: childcare, eldercare, housing, addiction support, community resources

**Confidentiality guarantee:** Acme receives only aggregate, anonymised utilisation data. Individual usage is never disclosed to managers, HR, or any other Acme employee.

## 5.3 Management Escalation Path

Before escalating to HR, employees are encouraged to follow the management chain where appropriate and safe to do so:

**Step 1 — Direct Manager:**
Raise the issue with your direct manager in a 1:1 meeting. Many workplace concerns (unclear expectations, workload, team friction) are resolved at this level. Document what was discussed and any agreed outcomes.

**Step 2 — Skip-Level Manager:**
If your direct manager is part of the problem, or if the issue remains unresolved after a reasonable timeframe (typically 1–2 weeks), escalate to your manager's manager. Notify your direct manager that you are doing so, unless doing so would create risk of retaliation.

**Step 3 — HR Business Partner:**
If the skip-level conversation does not resolve the issue, or if the matter involves conduct, discrimination, or a formal policy concern, contact your HRBP directly. HRBPs can advise informally or initiate a formal process as appropriate.

**Step 4 — HR Director or CHRO:**
For matters involving VP-level or above employees, systemic concerns, or if the HRBP handling is unsatisfactory, escalate to the HR Director or CHRO.

## 5.4 Formal Grievance Procedure

A formal grievance is a written complaint submitted to HR about a workplace issue that has not been resolved informally.

**Who can raise a grievance:**
Any current employee who has a concern about their working conditions, treatment by a colleague or manager, or a company policy decision that directly and adversely affects them.

**How to raise a formal grievance:**
1. Submit a written grievance to employeerelations@acme.com within **90 days** of the incident or last occurrence.
2. Include: a clear description of the issue, dates and specific incidents, names of relevant parties, any witnesses, and what outcome you are seeking.
3. HR will acknowledge receipt within **5 business days** and assign an investigator.

**Investigation process:**
- The investigator (HR or an independent party for senior-level cases) will speak with all relevant parties and review any relevant documents.
- Investigations are conducted confidentially. Information is shared only on a need-to-know basis.
- The investigating party may interview witnesses; witnesses must cooperate and maintain confidentiality.
- The employee raising the grievance may be accompanied at any formal meeting by a colleague or trade union representative.

**Outcome:**
- A written outcome will be provided within **30 business days** of the grievance being received (complex cases may take longer; the employee will be kept informed of any delays).
- Outcomes may include: corrective action against another party, policy clarification, mediation, workplace adjustments, or a finding of no breach of policy.

**Appeal:**
- If the employee is unsatisfied with the outcome, they may appeal in writing to the VP of People Operations within **10 business days** of receiving the outcome.
- The appeal will be reviewed by a different HR leader not involved in the original investigation.

**Retaliation is prohibited:** Any retaliation against an employee who raises a grievance in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence and will be investigated as such.

## 5.5 Anti-Harassment and Discrimination Reporting

Acme is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and bullying of any kind.

**Prohibited conduct includes:**
- Harassment based on race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, pregnancy, or any other protected characteristic.
- Quid pro quo harassment (conditioning employment decisions on unwelcome conduct).
- Hostile work environment (conduct severe or pervasive enough to interfere with work performance).
- Workplace bullying: repeated unreasonable behaviour that demeans, intimidates, or humiliates.

**How to report:**
- Speak to your HRBP or any HR team member.
- Email employeerelations@acme.com (treated as confidential; not shared with your direct manager).
- Use the **anonymous Ethics Hotline** at ethics.acme.com or call **1-800-555-SAFE** (available 24/7; reports are anonymous unless the reporter chooses to identify themselves).

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly. Substantiated violations result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 5.6 Conflict Resolution and Mediation

For interpersonal conflicts that cannot be resolved through direct conversation, Acme offers a structured mediation service before formal grievance escalation.

**What is mediation?**
A trained, neutral mediator facilitates a structured, confidential conversation between the parties in conflict. The goal is to reach a mutually agreed resolution, not to assign blame.

**How it works:**
1. Request mediation by emailing employeerelations@acme.com or speaking to your HRBP.
2. HR will check whether both parties consent to mediation (it is voluntary).
3. A mediator (internal HR professional or, for more complex cases, an independent external mediator) is appointed.
4. Separate pre-mediation meetings are held with each party to understand their perspective.
5. A joint mediation session (usually 2–4 hours) is facilitated by the mediator.
6. Any agreed outcomes are documented and shared with both parties; HR is notified only of the fact that mediation occurred and whether it was resolved.

**Confidentiality:** Nothing said in mediation is shared with management or used in any disciplinary or grievance process without the explicit consent of both parties.

**Timeline:** Mediation is typically arranged within **2 weeks** of request.

## 5.7 Whistleblower Policy and Protections

Acme encourages employees to report suspected fraud, financial irregularities, safety violations, data breaches, or other legal or ethical concerns.

**How to report:**
- Legal and Compliance team: compliance@acme.com
- Anonymous Ethics Hotline: ethics.acme.com or **1-800-555-ETHICS** (available 24/7)
- Direct report to the CHRO or General Counsel if the concern involves senior leadership

**Protections:**
- Employees who make a report in good faith are protected from any retaliation under Acme policy and applicable whistleblower protection laws.
- Retaliation against a whistleblower — including demotion, reassignment, termination, or hostile treatment — is a serious disciplinary offence and may expose the company and individuals to legal liability.
- Anonymity is preserved to the fullest extent possible when reports are made through the Ethics Hotline.

Reports are investigated by Legal & Compliance independently of HR where any conflict of interest exists. A summary outcome is provided to the reporter (where identifiable) within 60 days.

## 5.8 Disciplinary Process

For conduct or performance issues not covered by the grievance or PIP processes, Acme follows a progressive discipline framework:

1. **Verbal warning** — documented in the HR system by the manager; a copy given to the employee.
2. **Written warning** — formal document prepared with HR; signed by manager and employee; filed in personnel record.
3. **Final written warning** — issued where previous warnings have not led to improvement; may include demotion, duty changes, or suspension without pay pending investigation.
4. **Termination** — with contractual notice, unless gross misconduct (see below).

**Gross misconduct (examples):** Fraud, deliberate data breach, physical violence, serious harassment, under the influence of alcohol or drugs at work, or wilful destruction of company property. Gross misconduct may result in **summary dismissal** (without notice or pay in lieu) following a fair investigation and opportunity for the employee to respond.

At every stage, employees have the right to be accompanied by a colleague or trade union representative at any formal disciplinary meeting.
