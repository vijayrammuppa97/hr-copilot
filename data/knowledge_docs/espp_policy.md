# Acme Corp — Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) Policy
**Version 1.0 | Effective: January 1, 2025 | Owner: Finance & People Operations**

---

## 1. Overview

The Acme Corp Employee Stock Purchase Plan (ESPP) is a voluntary benefit program that allows eligible employees to purchase Acme Corp common stock at a discounted price through payroll deductions. The ESPP is designed to encourage employee ownership and align employee interests with company performance.

---

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who Can Participate

To be eligible for the ESPP, an employee must:

- Be a **permanent full-time or part-time employee** (minimum 20 hours per week)
- Have completed at least **90 days of continuous employment** at Acme Corp
- Not own (directly or indirectly) 5% or more of Acme Corp's outstanding stock

### 2.2 Who Is Not Eligible

The following are not eligible to participate:

- Contractors, consultants, and temporary workers
- Interns or co-op students
- Employees on unpaid leave of absence exceeding 90 consecutive days

---

## 3. Offering Period and Purchase Period

### 3.1 Offering Period

- The ESPP operates on **semi-annual offering periods**:
  - **Period 1:** January 1 — June 30
  - **Period 2:** July 1 — December 31

### 3.2 Enrollment Windows

Employees may enroll or make changes during the **enrollment window**, which opens **30 days before** each offering period begins:

- **Period 1 enrollment:** December 1 — December 31
- **Period 2 enrollment:** June 1 — June 30

### 3.3 Purchase Date

Shares are purchased automatically on the **last business day** of each offering period using accumulated payroll deductions.

---

## 4. Contribution and Discount

### 4.1 Payroll Deduction Rate

- Employees may contribute between **1% and 15%** of their eligible compensation per pay period
- Contributions are made via **after-tax payroll deductions**
- Contribution rate can be changed once per offering period during the open enrollment window
- Contributions may be stopped at any time; however, withdrawing mid-period means accumulated funds are returned without purchase

### 4.2 Eligible Compensation

Eligible compensation includes:

- Base salary or hourly wages
- Overtime pay
- Commissions

Eligible compensation **excludes**: bonuses, equity awards, severance, expense reimbursements, and fringe benefits.

### 4.3 Purchase Discount

Employees purchase shares at a **15% discount** from the lower of:

- The fair market value (FMV) of Acme Corp stock on the **first day** of the offering period, or
- The FMV on the **last day** (purchase date) of the offering period

This "look-back" feature means employees always get the better of the two prices, minus 15%.

**Example:**
- Stock price on January 1 (start): $100
- Stock price on June 30 (purchase): $120
- Lower price = $100
- Discount (15%) = $15
- **Employee purchase price = $85 per share**

---

## 5. Contribution Limits

### 5.1 IRS Annual Limit

Per IRS Section 423 rules, an employee may not purchase more than **$25,000 worth of stock** (based on FMV at the start of the offering period) in any calendar year.

### 5.2 Company Limit

Acme Corp also applies a company-level cap:

- Maximum contribution: **$10,000 per offering period** ($20,000 per calendar year)
- If payroll deductions would exceed this cap, contributions are automatically reduced

---

## 6. Enrollment Process

### 6.1 How to Enroll

1. Log in to the **HR Portal** at hrportal.acme.com
2. Navigate to **Benefits → Employee Stock Purchase Plan**
3. Select your contribution percentage (1%–15%)
4. Review and confirm your enrollment before the enrollment window closes

### 6.2 Auto-Enrollment

New eligible employees are **not** auto-enrolled. Participation is entirely voluntary and requires active enrollment each offering period if the employee is new or previously withdrew.

### 6.3 Re-enrollment

Existing participants are **automatically re-enrolled** in each new offering period at their previous contribution rate unless they make a change or withdraw.

---

## 7. Withdrawal and Refunds

### 7.1 Mid-Period Withdrawal

Employees may withdraw from the ESPP at any time during an offering period by submitting a withdrawal request in the HR Portal.

- All accumulated contributions are **refunded in full** within 2 pay periods
- No shares are purchased upon withdrawal
- Withdrawn employees may re-enroll in the next offering period

### 7.2 Termination of Employment

If an employee leaves Acme Corp (resignation, termination, or retirement) before the purchase date:

- Accumulated contributions are **refunded in full** within the final paycheck
- No shares are purchased

### 7.3 Death or Disability

In the event of death or permanent disability, the employee's estate or legal representative may:

- Request a full refund of contributions, or
- Request that shares be purchased on the next available purchase date using accumulated contributions

---

## 8. Tax Implications

### 8.1 General Tax Treatment

ESPP shares may qualify for **favorable tax treatment** under IRS Section 423 if the shares are held for the required holding period. Employees are strongly encouraged to consult a tax advisor.

### 8.2 Qualifying Disposition

A qualifying disposition occurs when shares are sold:

- More than **2 years** after the offering period start date, AND
- More than **1 year** after the purchase date

Tax treatment: The discount portion is taxed as **ordinary income**; any additional gain is taxed as **long-term capital gain**.

### 8.3 Disqualifying Disposition

A disqualifying disposition occurs when shares are sold before meeting the above holding periods.

Tax treatment: The full spread (FMV on purchase date minus purchase price) is taxed as **ordinary income** in the year of sale.

### 8.4 W-2 Reporting

Acme Corp will report ESPP income on the employee's **W-2** in the year of a disqualifying disposition.

---

## 9. Share Management

### 9.1 Brokerage Account

Purchased shares are deposited into the employee's account at **Acme's designated brokerage partner** (currently Fidelity). Employees must set up a brokerage account before their first purchase date.

### 9.2 Selling Shares

Employees may sell their shares at any time through the brokerage account. However, selling within the holding periods (see Section 8.2) will result in a disqualifying disposition.

### 9.3 Transferring Shares

Shares may be transferred to a personal brokerage account after the applicable holding period.

---

## 10. Plan Changes and Termination

### 10.1 Company Rights

Acme Corp reserves the right to:

- Amend, suspend, or terminate the ESPP at any time with 30 days' notice to participants
- Reduce the discount rate with notice
- Change offering period dates

### 10.2 Effect of Corporate Events

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or change of control:

- Outstanding offering periods may be shortened, with a purchase occurring before the event closes
- The Board of Directors will determine the appropriate treatment of in-progress contributions

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When can I first enroll?**
A: After 90 days of employment, during the next open enrollment window.

**Q: Can I change my contribution rate mid-period?**
A: You can only change your rate during the enrollment window. You may stop contributions entirely at any time.

**Q: What happens if the stock price drops below my purchase price?**
A: Because of the look-back feature and 15% discount, your purchase price is always 15% below the lower of the start or end price. You are always buying at a discount to market price.

**Q: Is there a minimum contribution?**
A: Yes, the minimum contribution is 1% of eligible compensation.

**Q: Can part-time employees participate?**
A: Yes, if they work at least 20 hours per week and have completed 90 days of service.

**Q: What if I go on parental or medical leave?**
A: Payroll deductions pause during unpaid leave. Accumulated contributions remain in the plan and are used for purchase on the purchase date if you are still employed.
